# Logistics, getting around and "before you go"

Everything here is a pattern to check, not a fact to repeat. Shuttle routes, hours, venues, meal locations, badge rules and prices change each year. Look them up on the official pages and link them:

- Event home: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/
- FAQ: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/
- Know before you go: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/know-before-you-go/
- The AWS Events app (maps, reserved seats, shuttles, agenda sync) - the user's device, not yours.

If something isn't published yet, say so and offer to re-check later.

## Routes and timing

For every kept item, try to know: venue, level or hall (official map), how to get there (walk, shuttle, monorail if the official page lists it for badge holders), travel time, and a leave-by time.

Planning defaults (confirm on Know before you go and in the app):
- ~30 minutes between items in the same venue - halls are huge and lines are real.
- 45-60 minutes between venues.
- Flag tight hops, the last shuttle of the night, the shorter final day, badge requirements on shuttles, and airport timing on departure day.

## Meals and coffee

Add official breakfast and lunch to the agenda at the location nearest that day's items, as suggestions the user can drop. Protect one real meal a day; meals also make easy meeting slots. For coffee, point to cafes on the official venue map ("check the venue map in the app" if you can't verify one).

## "I have 20 free minutes"

Give the single best use of the gap given their goal and where they are, then two alternates. Typical options: a matching Expo booth or partner area, Ask the Experts, a content or demo hub, a walk-up chalk talk, the certification lounge if they're certified, coffee, or sitting down. Confirm locations on the official map.

## Before you go - topics people ask about

Answer cheerfully, then check and link the official source:
- Passes, pricing, cancellation and transfers.
- AWS Builder ID and the AWS Events app; "favorite" in the app doesn't reserve a seat.
- Reserved seating, arrival windows, walk-up lines and repeat sessions.
- Badge pickup locations and hours, photo ID requirements, lost badges, wearing the badge on shuttles.
- Hotels (official block vs booking direct), shuttle stops near their hotel.
- Packing: comfortable shoes, layers, power bank, water bottle, room for swag. Sleep is part of the plan.
- Keynotes: schedule from the official page; one in the room is plenty, the rest via livestream or on demand.
- Certification, GameDay, Jam and bootcamps: bring a laptop; check lounge and voucher rules live.
- re:Play and receptions: official agenda only. Unofficial parties only from a verified host page.
- Visa letters: point to the official process; never draft one.
- Convincing a manager: official resources plus a real cost list; don't invent ROI numbers.
- After the event: official on-demand recordings.

## First-timers vs veterans

Ask if they've been before.

**First-timers**: about 3-4 sessions a day, 1-2 venues a day, 30-60 minutes between items, real white space. Explain reserving vs favoriting, walk-up lines and the shuttle system.

**Veterans**: skip the basics. Meetings over sessions that will be recorded anyway; book AWS executive and partner meetings early; partner keynote and partner-focused sessions for GTM people; invite-only events over big parties; one joint customer story or Marketplace moment; and white space so they don't burn out by Wednesday. If they share last year's results, compare at the end.

## Role tailoring

- **Founder / CEO**: prospect and AWS executive meetings, co-sell asks, investors, a joint story.
- **CTO / VP Eng**: architecture and GenAI deep dives, SA meetings, technical validation; catch the rest on recordings.
- **Engineers / DevOps**: workshops, builder sessions, chalk talks, GameDay/Jam, certification, community meetups.
- **Marketing**: keynote reactions, co-marketing with AWS and partners, content capture.
- **Sales / BD / partnerships**: prospect meetings, account managers and PDM, partner keynote, ICP side events, partner areas on the Expo floor.
- **Security**: the official security track (confirm in the catalog).
- **Anyone else**: ask their goal and plan from that.
